LONG-FORM POST (X Premium only)
================================================================

Spent today at the Generative UI Global Hackathon shipping a sales
workflow builder where the entire UI is generated by the agent at
runtime.

The setup: each company configures their workflow once (lead →
estimate → invoice, or whatever shape fits their process). After that,
every user request is handled by Gemini composing the right A2UI
surface — editable forms, line-item tables, action cards — instead of
routing the user to a static page.

Same chat input, two different companies onboarded → two completely
different rendered UIs. The agent owns the entire surface.

Stack: @CopilotKit's runtime with A2UI middleware on a Next.js route,
Gemini 2.5 Flash from @googledevs as the LLM, Supabase for workflow +
flow + submission state.

Most interesting bit: the backend doesn't know Gemini exists, and
Gemini doesn't know Supabase exists. The frontend bridges them with
four typed `useFrontendTool` handlers. Adding a new action is a
30-line PR.

DMs open if you're working on agentic UIs, generative interfaces, AI
for B2B tooling, or just shipping fast at hackathons — would love to
compare notes.

Thanks @aitinkerers for putting this on.

#genUIHackathon
